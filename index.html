    06 / LEARN

    How charging really works

    The essentials for accurate expectations.

    01

    Wattage isn't constant

    Fast charging usually slows as the battery fills. The final 10–20% often takes disproportionately longer.

    02

    The phone sets a limit

    Using a 100W charger with a 30W-rated phone will not automatically produce 100W charging.

    03

    Protocols matter

    USB PD, PPS, and proprietary methods may require different compatible chargers and cables.

    04

    Heat changes everything

    Gaming, direct sun, wireless-coil misalignment, and a hot battery can reduce charging power.
